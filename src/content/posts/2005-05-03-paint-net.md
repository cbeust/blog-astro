![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cbeust/beust-com/master/pics/bmw.jpg)

[Paint.net](http://www.eecs.wsu.edu/paint.net/) is a free replacement for MSPaint.  MSPaint is convenient because available on every Windows machine out there, but it's extremely primitive and for those of us who need a little bit more without requiring all the bells and whistles of Photoshop, it's not really an option.  After searching for a while, I finally found Paint.net which, despite a few glitches on my machine, seems to be very powerful while easy to use.
