There will be two EJB 3.0 events at TheServerSide Symposium this week:

  * Linda de Michiel will be making a presentation during the normal conference tracks Thursday at 3:30pm  
 
  * Some of the EJB 3.0 Experts Group members will be hosting a BOF session Friday night at 8:00pm (Linda de Michiel, Gavin King, Marc Fleury, Michael Keith and myself, and maybe a few others).  This will be the best time to come and ask your questions about the future of EJB 3.0.



Check out the [ full schedule](http://www.theserverside.com/symposium/schedule.html) and the [BOF schedule](http://wiki.theserverside.com/symposium/display/MEETINGS/Home) for more details.

Hope to see you all there!
