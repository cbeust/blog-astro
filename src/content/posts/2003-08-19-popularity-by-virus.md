So far, I have received over a hundred emails from people infected by the [new](http://www.f-secure.com/v-descs/sobig_f.shtml) [virus](http://www.sophos.com/virusinfo/analyses/w32sobigf.html), and receiving more every hour.  I took some time to see if I knew any of the originators of these emails, so that I could notify them they have been infected in case I happen to know them.  Nope.  All perfect strangers so far.

In some perverted way, you can measure how popular you are by how many infected emails you receive.

Corollary:  if you haven't received any infected email yet, nobody loves you.
