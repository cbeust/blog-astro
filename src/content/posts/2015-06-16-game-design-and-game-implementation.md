> "Because of a bug (it is an off-by-one error) the parley can only work if enemy and party member *do not* speak the same language." 

One of the many memorable quotes from a [fascinating article on an old school RPG](http://crpgaddict.blogspot.com/2015/05/1990-loose-end-1-legend-of-faerghail.html) by one of the developers. I don't even know this particular video game, even though I was playing a lot of RPG during my Apple ][ and Amiga days, but this particular quote resonates with me because it ties game design and game implementation together in a very explicit way. 

The whole article is a must read for anyone who's interested in game development or game design, or both.
