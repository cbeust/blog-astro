I am a big fan of [Strong Bad](http://homestarrunner.com).

Strong Bad has been stuck at his 99th email for a while now, and I was very much looking forward to reading the 100th.  Since it was still not appearing on the main page, I just went ahead and [typed the URL myself](http://homestarrunner.com/sbemail100.html).  Surprise... what you see looks like a 404, but don't be too quick to close the window and put your headphones on.

Finally, yesterday, the 100th email "officially" appeared on the main page and it is called "[Flashback](http://homestarrunner.com/sbemailahundred.html)".  Fortunately, they didn't use the same URL, so both emails are still available.

Happy anniversary, Strong Bad, keep up the good work.
